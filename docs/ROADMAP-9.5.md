# Raccoon Restoration — 10/10 Execution Roadmap

**Status:** This document supersedes the earlier score-first roadmap.
**Standard:** truthful, conversion-capable, accessible, technically resilient, and
distinctive enough to be judged against leading digital experiences—not merely other
contractor sites.

No award score can be guaranteed. The operating target is a site with no known trust,
legal, delivery, accessibility, or performance defect and with enough original proof
and craft to deserve top-tier consideration.

## Non-negotiable controls

- Nothing marked `pending` or `quarantined` in
  [`TRUTH-REGISTER.md`](TRUTH-REGISTER.md) is published as fact.
- Claim-adjusting, policy-interpretation, representation, negotiation, and settlement
  language remains quarantined until Illinois and Texas counsel approves the exact
  entities, relationships, licenses, and wording.
- Reviews, project outcomes, statistics, credentials, locations, hours, and warranties
  require source evidence and reuse approval.
- The production crowned-RR lockup is not redrawn, re-typeset, recolored, or
  reconstructed. Follow [`BRAND-ASSET-STANDARD.md`](BRAND-ASSET-STANDARD.md).
- A contact interaction reports success only after the lead is durably accepted and
  routed. Until then, the site must describe the email-client handoff honestly.
- Motion is progressive enhancement. Core content and conversion remain available
  when JavaScript, canvas, motion, or network dependencies fail.

## Phase 0 — Truth, trust, and delivery foundation

**Goal:** make the current experience safe to evaluate and impossible to mistake for a
production-ready lead system.

### Complete in this slice

- Contractor-safe public copy replaces unapproved claim-advocacy language.
- Fictional testimonials and unsupported statistics are removed.
- Speculative address, hours, geo, rating, and FAQ structured data are removed.
- The contact form validates clearly and uses an explicit email-client handoff.
- Reveal animation fails open.
- Logo, favicon, app-icon, and social-image uses track the production reference.
- Truth, owner-input, and brand-control documents are in the repository.
- Regression tests guard the trust and failure-mode fixes.
- Runtime and dependency inputs are pinned through `.nvmrc`, CI, and `package-lock.json`.

### Exit gate

The owner answers the blocker-ordered questions in
[`OWNER-INPUTS.md`](OWNER-INPUTS.md), assigns accountable owners, and supplies secure
evidence references. Every fact approved for public use is updated to `verified`.

### Local implementation checkpoint — 2026-07-27

The code-controllable foundation and responsive remediation are complete in the local
candidate:

- 80/80 automated checks pass across Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit;
- the exact supplied production raster lockup is used without redrawing it;
- mobile and tablet avoid fixed-content collisions and document-level overflow;
- the seven-service register, four-route Groundline brief, direct call path,
  email-client fallback, reduced-motion experience, live breakpoint behavior, and
  functional no-JavaScript alternatives are covered by regression tests; and
- public copy exposes only the verified Illinois license, Barrington city/state, BBB,
  GAF, phone, and email facts recorded in the Truth Register.

This checkpoint does not clear the owner, rights, counsel, CRM, privacy, staging,
deployment, manual assistive-technology, or real-project proof gates below.

## Phase 1 — Narrative and proof architecture

**Goal:** replace generic assurances with a concise, evidence-backed homeowner story.

1. Build one flagship project dossier from approved records:
   matched before/during/after media, system scope, materials, timeline, closeout
   documents, releases, and a verified homeowner voice.
2. Establish a clear page narrative:
   situation → contractor inspection → documented repair scope → restoration plan →
   build → closeout.
3. Add real proof modules only when their sources and permissions are recorded.
4. Build location/service pages from verified dispatch coverage and genuinely useful
   regional guidance—not doorway copy.
5. Keep the primary conversion choice simple: call or request an inspection.

### Exit gate

Every proof element maps to an evidence record; usability tests show that a homeowner
can understand the offer, service region, next step, and contact behavior without
guesswork.

## Phase 2 — Signature experience and art direction

**Goal:** turn the strong technical illustration language into one coherent,
recognizably Raccoon experience.

1. Refine the Storm Sequence as a directed story rather than decorative motion.
2. Use the raccoon character sparingly as a narrative payoff, never as false proof.
3. Extend the drafting language into a consistent damage/system illustration library.
4. Introduce approved, art-directed real project imagery when rights are complete.
5. Tune rhythm, typography, sound-off motion, transitions, and responsive compositions
   at 390, 834, 1440, and wide desktop sizes.
6. Preserve equivalent static and reduced-motion experiences.

### Exit gate

The sequence remains smooth on representative mid-tier hardware, does not delay core
content, and survives reduced-motion, Save-Data, no-canvas, no-JavaScript, and injected
initialization failures.

## Phase 3 — Local discovery and content depth

**Goal:** earn search visibility through verified local usefulness.

1. Confirm which locations are eligible for public address display and Google Business
   Profiles.
2. Reconcile legal name, phone, service regions, hours, and credentials across the site
   and owned profiles.
3. Publish location and service content only where operations can genuinely fulfill it.
4. Add concise homeowner guides grounded in contractor expertise and counsel-approved
   boundaries.
5. Keep structured data conservative and consistent with visible content.

### Exit gate

No NAP conflict, invented location, unsupported service area, thin doorway page, hidden
schema claim, or stale credential remains.

## Phase 4 — Durable conversion system

**Goal:** make lead delivery reliable, observable, private, and honest.

1. Integrate the approved CRM or lead service through a server-controlled endpoint.
2. Define required fields, optional marketing consent, access roles, retention,
   deletion, vendor ownership, and incident handling.
3. Implement idempotency, abuse controls, queue/retry behavior, dead-letter recovery,
   routing by region, and monitored alerts.
4. Test accepted, rejected, duplicate, timeout, vendor-outage, retry, and after-hours
   paths.
5. Use truthful pending, success, recoverable-failure, and fallback states.
6. Reconcile call routing and missed-call recovery with displayed availability.

### Exit gate

Synthetic leads reach the correct owner once, failures are recoverable, alerts have an
acknowledged owner, and the public privacy/contact disclosures match reality.

## Phase 5 — Award-grade polish and launch

**Goal:** close the gap between a strong build and a fully finished public experience.

### Quality gates

- **Truth:** zero unsupported public or structured-data claims.
- **Conversion:** phone and form paths pass end to end.
- **Accessibility:** automated checks plus keyboard, zoom, reduced-motion, VoiceOver,
  NVDA, and TalkBack spot checks.
- **Performance:** field-informed budgets for LCP, INP, CLS, total weight, and motion
  smoothness; no invented score claims.
- **Resilience:** core content and contact options survive dependency and enhancement
  failures.
- **SEO:** canonical, indexation, metadata, crawl assets, and verified local facts pass.
- **Visual craft:** responsive layouts, exact logo use, real-media rights, social cards,
  and cross-browser states pass.
- **Operations:** rollback, monitoring, analytics ownership, credential rechecks, and
  incident contacts are documented.

### Jury loop

Run independent design, usability, content, accessibility, performance, local-search,
conversion, privacy, and legal-boundary reviews against fresh evidence. An adversarial
verifier must reproduce each claimed pass. Fix confirmed findings, recapture, and repeat
until no launch-blocking defect remains.
