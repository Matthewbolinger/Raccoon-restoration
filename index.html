06 / Questions

Fair questions.

Straight answers to common homeowner questions. Have one we missed? Ask us directly.

I think my roof took hail or wind damage. What should I do first?

Don't climb up there. Call us and we can arrange an inspection with your permission, document the visible roof and exterior conditions, and explain the contractor-side repair options we see.

What happens after a storm inspection?

We organize visible conditions, measurements, materials, and repair considerations into a contractor-side scope. We can explain the construction details and discuss technical repair information with you and other project professionals.

What will I get from an inspection?

A clear explanation of the visible conditions, what appears to need attention, and the practical next step. The exact inspection deliverables and timing are confirmed when your appointment is arranged.

How long does storm restoration take?

Timing depends on the repair scope, material availability, weather, permits, and scheduling. After the inspection, we explain the sequence and confirm the project schedule before work begins.

Do you handle planned projects too?

Yes. The services shown here include planned retail projects such as roofing, siding, gutters, windows and doors, interior restoration, wallpaper, and paint. Confirm the specific scope with the team.

What warranty do you offer?

Warranty terms, when available, depend on the selected system, manufacturer, installer, and project agreement. Ask for the written material and workmanship terms that would apply before work begins.
