07

Fair questions

The honest answers we give at every kitchen table. Have one we missed? Ask us directly.

I think my roof took hail or wind damage. What should I do first?

Don't climb up there — call us. We'll inspect and photograph the roof, siding, and gutters for free, usually within 24–48 hours. If there's no meaningful damage, we'll tell you that too, in writing, and you'll have a documented baseline for the next storm.

What does a public adjuster actually do?

The insurance company's adjuster works for the insurance company. A licensed public adjuster works for you — documenting the full scope of loss, filing and managing the claim, and negotiating the settlement. Policyholders with professional representation routinely recover settlements that reflect the true cost of restoration rather than the first offer.

How much does an inspection cost?

Nothing. Inspections are free and no-obligation. You'll get an honest assessment and a photo report either way — education is part of the job, whether or not you hire us.

How long does storm restoration take?

Once a claim is approved, most roof replacements finish in one to two days; a full exterior restoration typically runs a few weeks depending on materials and scope. The claim itself is usually the longer road — which is exactly why we manage it for you and keep you updated at every milestone.

Do you only handle insurance work?

No — the same craftsmen handle retail projects: new roofs, siding, gutters, windows and doors, interior refinishing, wallpaper and paint. Insurance work is where we're rare; craftsmanship is where we live.

What warranty do you offer?

Manufacturer warranties on materials plus our own workmanship warranty, in writing, delivered at your final walkthrough. And because our commitment doesn't end when the work is done, you can call us about that roof for as long as you own it.
